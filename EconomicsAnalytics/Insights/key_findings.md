## 📦 Product Performance

### 📉 High Sales ≠ High Profit

Several high-volume products operate at only **~13–16% margins**, highlighting pricing and cost-optimization opportunities.

### 💎 Hidden Gems

Some lower-volume products achieve **45–50%+ margins**, suggesting opportunities for increased promotion and visibility.

### ↩️ Return Risk

Several major Electronics products have return rates above **20%**, creating significant refund and customer-experience exposure.

---

## 🔄 Retention

### 📈 Growing Customer Engagement

Monthly active customers increased from:

**123 → 2,330**

between January 2024 and August 2026.

### 🔁 Returning Customers

Returning-customer rate increased from **16.81%** in February 2024 to **79.36%** in August 2026.

> 🚀 This indicates substantially stronger customer re-engagement over time.

### 🧩 Cohort Retention

Recent cohorts show stronger early retention, with several 2026 cohorts achieving **25–40%+ Month-1 retention**.

> ⚠️ Recent cohorts have shorter observation windows, so their long-term retention should be monitored as they mature.
